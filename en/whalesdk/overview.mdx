title: WhaleSDK
description: Embed the complete trading experience into your own client
---

WhaleSDK delivers a fully implemented client trading UI with complete business functionality. Brokers integrate it into their own apps and mainly handle host integration, identity handoff, branding, and release — not rebuilding trading features.

It ships in three form factors:

- **iOS** — native delivery for broker iOS apps
- **Android** — native delivery for broker Android apps
- **WebTrade** — delivery for web or web-container hosts

## Responsibility boundary

WhaleSDK pages will always distinguish between:

- **Provided by WhaleSDK** — the trading UI and business functionality
- **Host app responsibilities** — integration, identity/session handoff, native permissions, release
