Whale Console is the back-office product used by broker operators for daily counter operations: review, configuration, enquiries, and exception handling. This section will host its operation manuals and task guides.

<Note>
This section is under construction. Operation guides are being produced together with the role-permission matrix.
</Note>

## Planned contents

- **Overview & Access** — sign-in, environment identification, roles / permissions / data scope, navigation
- **Customer & Account Operations** — onboarding review, customer search, account status maintenance
- **Funding Operations** — deposit / withdrawal review, transfers and FX, tracing fund movements
- **Trading Operations** — order and execution search, exceptional orders, position enquiries
- **Clearing, Risk & Compliance** — end-of-day tasks, reconciliation, risk alerts, compliance queues
- **Reports & Administration** — report export, user & role administration, parameter configuration, audit logs

Each task guide will state the required role permission, preconditions, outcome, audit impact, and exception handling.
