---
title: Historical Integration Q&A
description: Historical answers about account opening, cash adjustments, errors, and brokerage accounts
---

<Note>
This page preserves conclusions from earlier integration work. Environment addresses, rate limits, and business rules may vary by project; reconfirm them with the Whale project team before production.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does account opening take, and how often should Broker query?">
    If the project is configured for opening without manual approval, a background task still performs the operation. Duration depends on current concurrency. Broker may query every few seconds; confirm the actual interval and rate limit for the project.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the bizCode for deposits and withdrawals, and how is amount represented?">
    Refer to the [BrokerAPI asset endpoint](https://apidocs.longbridgewhale.com/whaleapi/asset-348068f0) for the business code. Send a positive amount for both deposits and withdrawals and distinguish the direction with `direction`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many decimal places do cash adjustments support?">
    At most two decimal places. Account balances are also stored with two decimal places.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should error handling use HTTP status or the response-body code?">
    Historical endpoints may return HTTP `400` for an error while the body contains a business code such as `404002` or `404102`. Use body `code` for business handling. A normal request returns HTTP `200` with body `code` equal to `0`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the UAT environment domain?">
    Historical material records `https://api.longbridgewhale.com`. Confirm the current project address with the Whale project team before configuring outbound access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Must Broker freeze funds before a cash adjustment?">
    No. Call the cash-adjustment endpoint directly and do not call the fund-freeze endpoint, which was not designed for adjustments. In the historical implementation, outgoing adjustments did not enforce risk controls and could exceed the available balance, so the caller had to enforce the limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Must Broker distinguish upstream counterparties for multiple markets?">
    No. Broker uses `account_no` and does not need to know the downstream counterparty. After cash enters the brokerage account, the Customer can exchange currency and trade US and Hong Kong securities according to the account configuration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
