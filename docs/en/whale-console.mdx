Whale Console is the back-office console for operating Longport Whale. Its manual covers day-to-day counter operations, review, configuration, and monitoring for the whole console.

<Note>
Access requires an approved IP allowlist. Whale enables allowlist access for a Broker after onboarding is complete, and the Whale Console application itself is also restricted to allowlisted IPs.
</Note>

## Before you can access {/* before-you-can-access */}

- Complete Broker onboarding. See [Broker onboarding](/en/docs/broker-onboarding).
- Whale enables IP allowlist access for your organization.
- Reach the console and its manual from an allowlisted network.

## Open the Whale Console manual {/* open-the-manual */}

Once your access is enabled, open the manual:

[Open the Whale Console manual](https://docs.longportwhale.com/en/docs/)

If the link does not open, your IP is likely not yet on the allowlist. Contact the project team to confirm your onboarding status and allowlist configuration.
